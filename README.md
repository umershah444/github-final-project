# Simple Interest Calculator

A small Bash script that calculates simple interest, written for a micro-finance
startup whose mission is to empower and provide opportunities to low-income individuals.

## What it does
The script `simple-interest.sh` asks for three inputs:
- **Principal**: the amount borrowed or invested
- **Rate of interest**: the annual rate, as a percentage
- **Time period**: the duration in years

It then calculates the interest using the formula:

    Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

## Usage
    chmod +x simple-interest.sh
    ./simple-interest.sh

## Example
    Enter the principal amount: 1000
    Enter the annual rate of interest (%): 5
    Enter the time period (years): 2
    Simple Interest = 100.00
